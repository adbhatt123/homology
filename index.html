Homology

In biology, homology describes similarities inherited from a common ancestor. A human hand and a bat’s wing: different functions, a shared foundation.

A common past connects them. A common purpose connects us.

Homology brings together AI researchers, scientists, engineers and industry experts with a shared ambition: to understand human biology more deeply and turn that understanding into ways to prevent, treat and ultimately cure disease.

We’re bringing together people so an unanswered question in biology can meet a new approach in AI. This is where the people studying life, building new tools and turning discoveries into treatments can exchange ideas, challenge each other and build together.

Because the missing piece in your work might be someone else’s life’s work.
